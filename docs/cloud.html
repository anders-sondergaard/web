Cloud

Access to the cloud

The cloud is your control center. Here you get a real-time overview of how your platform is performing.

Each Cinemataztic market has its own cloud, where all administration of the market’s platform takes place. Most markets share a login page and are taken to their own cloud after logging in. Some markets have their cloud on their own servers, and so have their own login page.

Need a password for your cloud? Contact CTO Allan Koudal at allan@cinemataztic.com.

On your Cinemataztic cloud you can

See statistics
Contact your users
Create and manage prizes
Monitor your machines
