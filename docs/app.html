Info

The Cinemataztic CineGame app, together with Cinemataztic’s DCH, is the cornerstone of the digital transformation of cinemas’ preshows.

The CineGame app is the connection between the screen and the guests. It makes it possible to add a wealth of digital earning opportunities and, of course, relevant entertainment for your guests.

All Cinemataztic apps are available for iPhone and Android.

Tailored to your market

Cinemataztic delivers a white-label app, tailored for each market. The app is the cornerstone for interactivity and audience profiling.
