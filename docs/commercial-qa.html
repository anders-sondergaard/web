Commercial Q&A

Are you in the process of launching a new market? Here are some of the most common questions.

How much will it cost to translate a CineGame to a new market?

Using the McDonald’s game as an example: approx. 4,000 EUR.

It always depends on how old the game is (software update), new prizes etc.

BonusSpin: How many questions can be included in the BonusSpin?

1 question.

BonusSpin: Who owns the personal user data?

The client owns the data. Cinemataztic hosts the site on behalf of the client.

BonusSpin: Can we delay the push with the voucher until after the movie?

Yes.

How many players can take part in a CineGame?

Most games allow an unlimited number of players. Runner games (Ben & Jerry’s, JD Sports) and Tivoli have a maximum of 15–20 players.

What is the production cost?
  • Type 1: 8,000 EUR, e.g. McDonald’s and Fanta Halloween
  • Type 2: 12,500 EUR, e.g. TV2 Play, New Can and DFDS
  • Type 3: 18,250 EUR, e.g. JD Sports, Tivoli and Samsung

See the games in Cases.

How many clients buy both regular advertising and CineGame?

70% of the CineGame clients don’t buy regular advertising. Example from Denmark: Podimo was a non-cinema client before they bought CineGame, and moved all their spend to CineGame.

Can all cinemagoers get the voucher/prize without taking part in a game?

No, only users/players can receive the voucher.

How many of the cinemagoers play?

The share of the audience playing the CineGame increases every year. It starts around 4% and ends around 15–20%.

How fast the number of players grows depends on in-cinema promotion of the game, its placement in the preshow and how good the cinema staff are at opening the doors to the auditorium before the CineGame starts.

What is the price per user?

Approx. 1 EUR per player.

What happens when the internet connection is bad?

With 4G and 5G it is not a problem. If the connection is bad, you see it on your avatar, so you don’t think there are issues with the game.

Where can I see examples of previous games and old pitches?

See Cases: filter by CineGame for games and by Pitch for pitches.
