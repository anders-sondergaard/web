Rewards

Reward options

All Cinemataztic products can award prizes:
A CineGame always awards a prize to the winner of the game but can also award prizes to anyone who participates. CineCard and Programmatic can hand out prizes to everyone who has seen them, and you can win prizes in all CineFun products

Rewards

Prizes

Prize formats

  1. Wallet

    Wallet
  2. Swipe
  3. Barcode
  4. 89FL75QX
    Code
  5. Lottery

Wallet:
The Wallet, the place all prizes awarded by a Cinemataztic product can be seen. An intuitive design makes it easy to see what the prize is, when it expires and where it can be redeemed.

Swipe:
Swipe prize, a prize with an intuitive swipe function that allows the prize to be used only once. After a Swipe, the prize changes state and can no longer be used.

Barcode:
A prize with a barcode or a QR code is an effective way to redeem prizes in physical stores. Cinemataztic supports all types of barcodes. It can be both a static or a generic barcode.

Code:
A prize with a code consisting of alphanumeric characters is often used for prizes that can be redeemed in online stores. It can be both a static or a generic code.

Lottery prize:
A Lottery prize cannot be redeemed. The winner of a Lottery prize participates in a raffle. The winner of the draw will be contacted.

CineGame prizes

Two options:

Option 1.
Only one prize per game (the winner will get a prize voucher in his/her In-app wallet)
Example:
Disneyland Paris:

  • Winner: A ticket in the lottery of winning a family weekend stay at Disneyland Paris

Option 2.
One prize for the winner + a smaller prize for all players
Example:
Boozt.com (online fashion brand):

  • Winner: 30 EUR cash discount (min. purchase: 75 EUR)
  • All players: 25% discount (min. purchase: 85 EUR)

A CineGame prize always consists of:

  • Information about what the prize consists of
  • A picture of the prize
  • A background color
  • Redemption location information
  • An expiration date or duration (Ex. 60 days)

A CineGame prize can include:

  • A QR or barcode
  • An alphanumeric code
  • Link to online redemption
Rewards

GoldCoins

Every time you use your CineGame app you will be rewarded.

  • If you participate in a CineGame you will get a Gold Coin, if you win - you get 5.
  • If you use a CineFun product, you will get a Gold Coin.

The gold coins can be used in the CineGames CoinShop

Rewards

CoinShop

In CineGames CoinShop, you can “buy” products from the cinema and their partners with your Gold Coins. It can be popcorn, sodas, discounts on movie tickets, value coupons for both physical stores and online stores.

Rewards

Samples

The cinema and their partners can send digital samples to all users of their app. This is a powerful tool for driving traffic to both cinemas, shops and online sellers. A Sample works in the same way as a CineGame prize, it is just distributed to all users or to a specific target group. All recipients of a sample receive a push message that draws attention to it.
