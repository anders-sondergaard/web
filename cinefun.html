CineGame+

CineGame+ is a range of casual games in the CineGame app, sponsored by an advertiser. The audience can play whenever they want, before and after the cinema visit, and win prizes from the advertiser.

Users get a notification when a new game arrives in their Game Center. Up to 35% of them try the game, and they spend an average of 12 minutes on it.

In-app gaming

Trailerquiz

The TrailerQuiz promotes upcoming movies by turning the movie trailer into a short multiple-choice trivia quiz. It is often done as a co-lab with a distributor and sponsored by an advertiser.

Users earn gold coins by watching trailers and answering quiz questions about them, and exchange the coins for products in the concession. That creates traffic for your movie theater.

Promote your long-format content

If you have long-form content that’s too costly to advertise through traditional channels, TrailerQuiz has you covered. Users watch your video, answer three quiz questions, and in return, they receive a prize like a discount code or lottery ticket. It’s a fun, engaging way to get your content noticed.

Brands & movies

If your brand is linked to a movie through product placement or other partnerships, TrailerQuiz can help you promote both. Users who complete the quiz can enter a lottery for a chance to win movie tickets, boosting engagement with both the brand and the movie.
