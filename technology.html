Technology

What’s next?

The future success of the cinema media depends on a successful digital transformation of the cinema experience and the creation of new digital revenue streams.

Connecting the audience

All Cinemataztic products are powered by the Digital Cinema Hub, the CineGame App and the Cinemataztic Cloud.

With the Cinemataztic platform the cinema journey is digitized and the cinema screen is transformed to an interactive space.

The digital transformation

The Cinemataztic platform is a plug & play solution delivering a digital infrastructure from where all user interactions and digital content are processed.

The Digital Cinema Hub is a hardware unit integrated with the cinema screen-server providing content to the cinema screen. The CineGame app is gateway of user interactions, data collection and content.

The hub and the app are connected by a unique 4-digit code displayed on each cinema screen.

Cinemataztic deliver a white-label app – to be tailored for each market. The app is the corner stone for interactivity and audience profiling.

CineGame in your preshow?

No cinemas are the same – and the Cinemataztic platform allow you to place the CineGame wherever you see fit. It can be before the regular preshow starts – as it is done in cinemas in Denmark & Finland. It can be a combination of starting before the announce start-time and in the beginning of the preshow – as it is done in Germany.
