    The audience downloads the CineGame app, available in the App Store and on Google Play, and enters the 4-digit code shown on the cinema screen to join the game.

    The CineGame is needed to capture the audience data used for programmatic targeting and voucher distribution.

    We accept MP4 (H.264) ads in 16:9 (1920×1080p) with a maximum video bitrate of 13 Mbit/s, and either stereo audio at a maximum volume of −4 dB or 5.1 surround sound with a maximum sound level of 80 dB.
