User journey

Step by step

From the cinema screen to a new lead in three steps: the ad, the voucher and the wheel of fortune.

  1. 01

    Play a game or watch an ad

    The cinema guest plays the game & watches a programmatic ad on the cinema screen.

  2. 02

    Receive a voucher

    Once engaging in a game or being exposed to a programmatic ad the guest receives a digital voucher in the CineGame app.

  3. 03

    Spin to win

    The voucher links to the lead site where the user can join a brand specific competition by accepting the advertiser's permission and share contact information, thus becoming a new lead to the advertiser.
