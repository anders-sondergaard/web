How it works

Join. Play. Win.

It takes less than a minute, and you can play from your seat while the cinema fills up.

  1. Get the app

    Download CineGame, or the app for your country, before you go to the cinema.

  2. Enter the code

    The game appears on the big screen with a 4-digit code. Type it in the app and you’re in.

  3. Play and win

    The whole auditorium plays at the same time. The best players win prizes, straight into the app.

Prizes

Win your snacks

The games are sponsored by brands, and so are the prizes. Winners get a voucher in the app, most often for something at the cinema concession, like popcorn or a soda. Show it at the counter and enjoy.

Good to know

Something else? Go to Support.

Which app should I download?

It depends on where you go to the cinema. Pick your country at the top of this page and we’ll show you the right app.

Where do I find the code?

On the cinema screen, before the movie. When a CineGame is about to start, a 4-digit code is shown on the big screen.

How do I get my prize?

Prizes land as vouchers in the app after the game. Most can be collected at the cinema concession: just show the voucher at the counter before it expires.

I need help with the app

Our support team is happy to help. Visit support.cinegame.com.
