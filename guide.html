Programmatic cinema

How it works

Before you create your campaign, it’s important to understand the core features and mechanics of programmatic cinema advertising.

Preferred deals

Programmatic cinema runs exclusively as preferred deals with no guaranteed delivery. You only pay for the impressions that actually get delivered. That makes budgets easier to manage and lets you scale up or down as needed.

First-price auction

Campaigns are delivered on the first-price auction principle: the highest bid wins the inventory and is charged at the bid price. You can adjust your bid percentage at any time during the campaign to optimise delivery and performance.

Cost per mille (CPM)

Every campaign gets a base-CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions). It may already be pre-negotiated between you and your cinema sales house, and is then your campaign’s floor price. If the pre-set CPM doesn’t match your agreed terms, you can suggest a revised CPM during setup.

Adjustments to base-CPM

The base-CPM adjusts automatically based on your campaign specifications:

  • Add a voucher to your campaign: +25%
  • Deselect cinemas: +5 EUR per cinema
  • Select ‘Family’ as target audience: −20%
  • Increase your bid percentage to compete for delivery
Create your campaign

6 easy steps

Follow these steps to set up your programmatic cinema campaign.

  1. 01

    Spot details

    Choose the number of spots and the spot duration.

  2. 02

    Creatives

    Drag and drop your MP4 video onto the platform. Choose voucher yes or no: with yes, you order your voucher and get an email when it’s ready.

  3. 03

    Day & time

    Enter the campaign start and end date, and pick specific days and time slots.

  4. 04

    Audience

    Target by demographics such as ‘Adults’ (A12+) and ‘Family’ (family movies), or refine with gender, age or movie genres.

  5. 05

    Distribution

    Set the geographic scope of your campaign by choosing the cinemas that match your campaign goals.

  6. 06

    Budget

    Enter your bid percentage for the first-price auction and your maximum campaign budget, and suggest a new base-CPM if needed.

Key feature

Digital vouchers

Programmatic cinema advertising includes a unique voucher feature, designed to deliver measurable low-funnel impact.

How it works

When your cinema spot is played, a digital voucher can be delivered directly to people in the audience.

Who receives it?

Vouchers are distributed to all audience members who played the CineGame at the beginning of the preshow.

Delivery

Vouchers are stored in the CineGame app wallet, so they’re easy to find and redeem.

This bridges cinema advertising with direct audience engagement, and opens up new conversion opportunities.

Voucher content options

  • Discount codes (unique or generic)
  • Deep links to the advertiser’s app
  • Links to competitions for newsletter sign-ups
  • Registration for call-center leads

Before you go live

Review & approval

Before your campaign is set live on the cinema screen, spot content and quality is reviewed and approved by the cinema sales house.

Content review

Spot quality and adherence to cinema advertising standards.

CPM approval

If you suggested a new base-CPM, it must also be reviewed and approved.

Campaign status

Approved

Once approved, your campaign status changes to ‘Approved’.

Not approved

If your campaign needs adjustments, you’ll get an email with the necessary updates.

Real-time data

Dashboard

Once your campaign is approved and live, you can track its performance in real time in the campaign dashboard.

Metrics tracked

  • Impressions delivered
  • Vouchers delivered
  • CVR% impressions / voucher
  • Budget spent

The dashboard updates after every cinema show where your spot has been played, so your campaign insights are always accurate and up to date.

Use the data to monitor delivery, and adjust your bid or strategy if needed to optimise performance.
